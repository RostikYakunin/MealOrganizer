# Meal Organizer: Your Personalized Meal Planning Companion

**Meal Organizer** is an innovative app designed to transform the way you approach meal planning and
nutrition. Whether you're aiming to optimize your diet, discover new recipes, or track your 
nutritional progress, Meal Organizer is your go-to tool for achieving your dietary goals 
with ease and precision.

## Key Features (Planned)

- **Smart Nutrition Calculator**  
  Effortlessly calculate the nutritional content of your meals based on the ingredients you input. 
Tailor your meals to meet your specific dietary needs, ensuring you get the right balance of 
nutrients every time.

- **Custom Menu Creation**  
  Build a personalized menu or meal plan from your preferred ingredients. 
Whether you’re working with what’s in your pantry or planning for the week ahead, Meal Organizer 
helps you craft delicious and nutritious meals.

- **Personalized Recipes**  
  Adjust recipes to match your dietary preferences, such as vegan or non-vegan options. 
Customize meals to suit your lifestyle and taste, making healthy eating more enjoyable.

- **Advanced Meal Filtering**  
  Filter meals based on preparation time and nutritional content. 
Whether you're in a hurry or looking to optimize your nutrient intake, find the perfect recipe 
that fits your schedule and goals.

- **Personal Progress Tracking**  
  Keep track of your daily and weekly nutritional intake. 
Monitor your progress toward goals like weight loss, maintenance, or gain. 
Visualize your achievements and make informed adjustments to your diet as needed.

- **Automated Shopping Lists**  
  Generate shopping lists based on your selected recipes with a single tap. 
Save your lists, track what you already have in stock, and streamline your grocery shopping experience.

- **Social Sharing and Inspiration**  
  Share your favorite recipes and dishes with friends or on social networks. 
Get inspired by others and expand your culinary repertoire through a vibrant community of food enthusiasts.

- **Comprehensive Diet Analysis**  
  Activate the diet analysis feature to receive tailored recommendations for a balanced diet. 
Identify nutrient gaps in your daily or weekly intake, and discover recipes that help you achieve a well-rounded diet.

- **Notifications and Reminders**  
  Stay on track with meal reminders and hydration prompts. 
Align your notifications with your personal goals to maintain consistency in your dietary habits.

---

**Meal Organizer** is more than just a meal planner—it's your partner in healthy living, 
designed to make your journey toward a balanced diet simple, enjoyable, and effective. 
Whether you’re a seasoned chef or just getting started in the kitchen, Meal Organizer empowers you 
to take control of your nutrition, one meal at a time.
